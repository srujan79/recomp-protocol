Supplement Protocol

Worth adding: Vitamin D3 (limited sun from a desk job) and a flax/chia or algae-based omega-3, since a vegetarian diet skips the usual fish source.

Air Fryer Rotation

High-protein, low-oil, low-effort — everything here is under 15 minutes active + cook time, using stuff already in your kitchen or gym-bag pantry. Macros are per serving.

01 — Paneer Tikka 10 min · Easy

245 kcal · 22g protein · 6g carbs · 15g fat

  1. Cube 150g paneer, roughly chop 1 onion + 1 capsicum into similar-size pieces.
  2. Toss everything in 2 tbsp curd + 1 tsp tandoori masala + ½ tsp oil + salt (skip marinating time if rushed — even a quick toss works).
  3. Skewer or spread in the basket. Air-fry at 190°C for 10 min, flipping/shaking once at the 5-min mark.
02 — Crispy Soya Chunks 10 min · Cheapest protein

190 kcal · 26g protein · 9g carbs · 4g fat (per 100g dry chunks)

  1. Boil a handful of soya chunks 5 min, drain and squeeze out all water firmly (this is the only step that matters for crispiness).
  2. Toss in curd + tandoori masala + 1 tsp oil.
  3. Air-fry at 200°C for 10 min, shaking the basket halfway.
03 — Sprouts Chaat 8 min · No real cooking

160 kcal · 14g protein · 20g carbs · 3g fat

  1. Toss a cup of moong sprouts with chopped onion, tomato, chaat masala, lemon, and a few drops of oil.
  2. Air-fry 6–8 min at 190°C just to add crunch and warm it through.
  3. Toss again and eat — works cold too if you skip the air fryer step entirely.
04 — Besan Chilla Rolls 10 min · Easy

180 kcal · 12g protein · 22g carbs · 6g fat

  1. Mix besan + water + turmeric + salt + chopped onion into a thin batter.
  2. Pour a thin layer onto parchment in the air-fryer basket.
  3. Air-fry 8–10 min at 180°C, then roll with mint chutney.
05 — Tandoori Tofu Skewers 12 min · Easy

210 kcal · 18g protein · 7g carbs · 12g fat

  1. Press tofu briefly to remove excess water, cube it.
  2. Marinate in curd + tandoori masala for 10 min (optional — still good without it).
  3. Skewer with bell peppers, air-fry 190°C for 12 min, turning once.
06 — Roasted Makhana & Papad 5 min · Zero effort

120 kcal · 4g protein · 18g carbs · 4g fat

  1. Toss a cup of makhana (foxnuts) with a few drops of ghee and a pinch of chaat masala.
  2. Air-fry 5 min at 160°C, shaking once.
  3. Roast a papad separately for 90 seconds — replaces the biscuit-tin habit at tea time.
07 — Sweet Potato Wedges 15 min · Post-leg-day carb refill

160 kcal · 3g protein · 34g carbs · 2g fat

  1. Cut one sweet potato into wedges, toss with ½ tsp oil, paprika, and salt.
  2. Air-fry 180°C for 15 min, shaking the basket halfway through.
08 — Egg Bhurji + Toast 7 min · Easy

280 kcal · 24g protein · 18g carbs · 13g fat

  1. Scramble 3 eggs stovetop with onion, tomato, chili, and turmeric (2–3 min — this part isn't air-fried).
  2. While that cooks, air-fry 2 slices of multigrain bread at 180°C for 3 min instead of buttering on a pan.
09 — Egg White Bites 10 min · Near-zero fat

130 kcal · 18g protein · 3g carbs · 4g fat

  1. Whisk 4 egg whites with chopped spinach/capsicum, salt, and pepper.
  2. Pour into silicone muffin cups (or a small oven-safe bowl).
  3. Air-fry 160°C for 10 min. Make a batch of 6 on Sunday, grab 2–3 as a snack all week.
10 — Paneer & Corn Cutlets 12 min · Easy

220 kcal · 16g protein · 16g carbs · 11g fat

  1. Mash 100g paneer with a handful of boiled corn, 1 tbsp besan, and spices.
  2. Shape into 4–5 small patties.
  3. Air-fry 190°C for 10–12 min, flipping once.
11 — Rajma Tikki 12 min · Uses leftover rajma

200 kcal · 15g protein · 24g carbs · 5g fat

  1. Mash a cup of boiled/leftover rajma with chopped onion, 1 tbsp besan, and spices.
  2. Shape into patties.
  3. Air-fry 190°C for 12 min, flipping halfway.
12 — Stuffed Capsicum (Paneer + Chana) 12 min · High protein

250 kcal · 20g protein · 15g carbs · 13g fat

  1. Halve a capsicum and deseed it.
  2. Mix crumbled paneer + boiled chana + spices, stuff into the halves.
  3. Air-fry 180°C for 10–12 min until the capsicum softens slightly.
13 — Curd-Marinated Mushroom Skewers 8 min · Low-cal

90 kcal · 10g protein · 5g carbs · 3g fat

  1. Marinate whole button mushrooms in curd + tandoori masala for 10 min (or skip and go straight in).
  2. Skewer, air-fry 190°C for 8 min, shaking once.
14 — Multigrain Protein Sandwich 6 min · Fastest one here

300 kcal · 20g protein · 28g carbs · 11g fat

  1. Mash a boiled egg or paneer with a little curd and spices, spread between 2 slices of multigrain bread.
  2. Brush the outside lightly with oil, air-fry 180°C for 6 min, flipping once.
Mind

Focus & Productivity System

Study placement

The 11–1 AWS meeting with your friend is the fixed anchor — everything else is built around it. A 1h40 solo block right after the gym (9:20–11:00) leads into it, and a 20-min evening Anki top-up closes the day at ~4h total on training days, ~2h10 solo on office days.

Deep work protocol

75-minute blocks. Phone in another room, single browser tab, Slack/email closed. After each video or reading, close it and write 5 sentences summarizing it from memory — active recall beats re-watching.

Spaced repetition

Build one Anki deck for AWS services, limits, and exam terms. 10–15 minutes daily, even on rest days — this is what makes Phase 3 exam-ready instead of a re-cram.

Beating the 5 PM slump

Never pair carb-only lunch with no protein — always chapati + dal/curd together. Swap the coffee-break biscuits for roasted chana or a boiled egg. Take the walk outdoors; daylight resets alertness better than a second coffee. No caffeine after 5:30 PM.

Sleep is non-negotiable

6.5–7h on training nights is the tight point in this plan — protect the 12:15 AM lights-out like a meeting you can't move. Screens dimmed 30 min prior, wind-down happens off the bed, and Sunday's later wake repays the week's debt.

Sunday planning ritual

2:00–4:00 PM: log the week's lifts, batch-prep 2–3 air-fryer proteins, and pick specific AWS modules/labs for the coming week — never open the week with "study AWS" as the plan.
